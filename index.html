Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026 Version 1.0

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what data the "Dr Strong" app collects, how it's used, who it's shared with, and how you can control it. By using the app, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account and use the app, we may collect the following data:

Data typeExamples
Account dataEmail address, password (encrypted)
Profile dataName, date of birth, gender, country, profile photo
Health & fitness dataSteps, fluids, fasting, calories, carbs/protein/fat, weight, activities
Food photosPhotos you take for AI-based calorie estimation
Food search dataSearch terms and barcode numbers you enter
LocationWhen creating or joining a group activity (optional)

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To display your stats and progress in the app (charts, goals, history).
  • To securely store your data so it's available whenever you sign in, on any device.
  • To estimate calories and macronutrients from food photos, barcodes, or text search.
  • To enable social features (steps challenge, group activities) when you choose to participate.

3. Who We Share Your Data With

We never sell your data to anyone. We only share limited data with services necessary to power the following features:

ServicePurposeWhat's shared
Firebase (Google)Account and data storage, sign-inAll account and app data
Anthropic (Claude)Analyzing food photos to estimate caloriesThe captured photo only, at time of use
Open Food FactsFood search and barcode lookupSearch term or barcode number only
Food photos and search queries are not permanently stored or linked to your identity by these third-party services — they are used only in the moment, to return a result inside the app.

4. Data Security

  • Sign-in and passwords are managed via Firebase Authentication; passwords are never stored as plain text.
  • Your personal data is tied to your account only and cannot be accessed by other users.
  • Data you choose to share (like a steps challenge) is only visible to participants who explicitly joined.

5. Your Rights

  • Access: You can review your data at any time within the app.
  • Correction: You can edit your profile and data directly from the Settings page.
  • Deletion: You can request permanent deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us below.

6. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from this age group. If we become aware that we have collected data from a child under this age, we will delete it promptly.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes to the app or legal requirements. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent revision, and we encourage you to review it periodically.
