DR STRONG Privacy Policy Last updated: 16 September 2026 · Version 1.1 Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what data the "Dr Strong" app collects, how it's used, who it's shared with, and how you can control it. By using the app, you agree to the practices described below. 1. Information We Collect When you create an account and use the app, we may collect the following data: Data type Examples Account data Email address, password (encrypted). You may also use the app with an anonymous account and no email at all. Profile data Name, date of birth, sex, country, height, weight, profile photo Health & fitness data Steps, fluids, fasting, calories, carbohydrates/protein/fat, weight, body measurements, nutrient readings you enter from your lab results Apple Health data Your step count and walking distance, read from Apple Health with your permission. Read only — we never write to Apple Health. Motion data The live step counter while you walk, from the device's motion sensor Food photos Photos you take for AI-based calorie estimation Barcode data Barcode numbers you scan from packaged products Some of this is sensitive health data — lab readings, weight and body measurements in particular. We do not sell it, do not use it for advertising, and do not share it with insurers or commercial parties. 2. What We Do Not Collect Your location. The app does not request or use location, and the iOS location permission is not declared at all. No advertising. There are no ads and no ad networks in the app. No tracking or behavioural analytics, and no advertising identifiers. 3. How We Use Your Data To display your stats and progress in the app (charts, goals, history). To securely store your data so it's available whenever you sign in, on any device. To estimate calories and macronutrients from food photos or barcodes. To enable social features (steps challenge, step groups) when you choose to participate. 4. Who We Share Your Data With We never sell your data to anyone. We only share limited data with services necessary to power the following features: Service Purpose What's shared Firebase (Google) Account and data storage, sign-in All account and app data Google (Gemini, via Firebase AI Logic) Analysing food photos to estimate calories The captured photo only, at time of use Open Food Facts Packaged-product lookup by barcode The barcode number only — nothing about you GitHub Pages Downloading recipes and food lists A file request — nothing about you jsDelivr Loading the barcode-reading engine the first time you scan A file request — nothing about you Google Fonts Interface fonts A font file request Food photos and barcode lookups are not permanently stored or linked to your identity by these third-party services — they are used only in the moment, to return a result inside the app. 5. What Other Users Can See Nothing by default. Nothing is shared unless you join a step group or a challenge yourself. When you do, only the members of that group can see your name and four step figures: today, this week, this month, and your streak. Your profile photo is not shared. Members see a single letter from your name in its place. Your weight, calories, body measurements and lab readings are never shared — not with the group, not with anyone. You can leave a group at any time, and your data disappears from it. 6. Data Security Sign-in and passwords are managed via Firebase Authentication; passwords are never stored as plain text. Your personal data is tied to your account only and cannot be accessed by other users. Data you choose to share (like a steps challenge) is only visible to participants who explicitly joined. 7. Device Permissions You can withdraw any permission at any time in iOS Settings. The app keeps working with whatever remains — the live step counter runs even if you refuse Apple Health. Permission Why Apple Health Reading your step count and distance. Read only. Motion & Fitness The live step counter while you walk. Camera Scanning barcodes and taking food photos. No image is saved from a barcode scan. Photos Choosing a profile picture. We do not read your library; you pick one image. Notifications Water, step and measurement reminders. Entirely optional. 8. Your Rights Access: You can review your data at any time within the app. Correction: You can edit your profile and data directly from the Settings page. Deletion: You can permanently delete your account and your personal data from inside the app — Profile → Delete account. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Data in groups you joined: The rows you published into a step group or a challenge (your name and step figures) are held in that group, not in your account, and deleting the account does not remove them. Leave the group first if you want them gone, or email us and we will remove them for you. A copy of your data: Email us at the address below and we will send it to you. 9. Children's Privacy This app is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from this age group. If we become aware that we have collected data from a child under this age, we will delete it promptly. 10. Food Data Sources USDA FoodData Central — whole foods. Public domain (CC0 1.0).
