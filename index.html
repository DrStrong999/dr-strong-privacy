Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026 · Version 1.2

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what data the "Dr Strong" app collects, how it's used, who it's shared with, and how you can control it. By using the app, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account and use the app, we may collect the following data:

Data typeExamples
Account dataEmail address and password (encrypted), or the Apple or Google account you choose to sign in with.
Profile dataName, date of birth, sex, country, height, weight, profile photo
Health & fitness dataSteps, fluids, fasting, calories, carbohydrates/protein/fat, weight, body measurements, nutrient readings you enter from your lab results
Apple Health dataYour step count and walking distance, read from Apple Health with your permission. Read only — we never write to Apple Health.
Motion dataThe live step counter while you walk, from the device's motion sensor
Food photosPhotos you take for AI-based calorie estimation
Barcode dataBarcode numbers you scan from packaged products
Some of this is sensitive health data — lab readings, weight and body measurements in particular. We do not sell it, do not use it for advertising, and do not share it with insurers or commercial parties.

2. What We Do Not Collect

  • Your location. The app does not request or use location, and the iOS location permission is not declared at all.
  • No advertising. There are no ads and no ad networks in the app.
  • No tracking or behavioural analytics, and no advertising identifiers.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To display your stats and progress in the app (charts, goals, history).
  • To securely store your data so it's available whenever you sign in, on any device.
  • To estimate calories and macronutrients from food photos or barcodes.
  • To enable social features (steps challenge, step groups) when you choose to participate.

4. Who We Share Your Data With

We never sell your data to anyone. We only share limited data with services necessary to power the following features:

ServicePurposeWhat's shared
Firebase (Google) Account and data storage, sign-in All account and app data
Google (Gemini, via Firebase AI Logic) Analysing food photos to estimate calories The captured photo only, at time of use
Open Food Facts Packaged-product lookup by barcode The barcode number only — nothing about you
GitHub Pages Downloading recipes and food lists A file request — nothing about you
jsDelivr Loading the barcode-reading engine the first time you scan A file request — nothing about you
Google Fonts Interface fonts A font file request

Food photos and barcode lookups are not permanently stored or linked to your identity by these third-party services — they are used only in the moment, to return a result inside the app.

5. What Other Users Can See

Nothing by default. Nothing is shared unless you join a step group or a challenge yourself. When you do, only the members of that group can see your name and four step figures: today, this week, this month, and your streak.

Your profile photo is not shared. Members see a single letter from your name in its place.

Your weight, calories, body measurements and lab readings are never shared — not with the group, not with anyone. You can leave a group at any time, and your data disappears from it.

6. Data Security

  • Sign-in and passwords are managed via Firebase Authentication; passwords are never stored as plain text.
  • Your personal data is tied to your account only and cannot be accessed by other users.
  • Data you choose to share (like a steps challenge) is only visible to participants who explicitly joined.

7. Device Permissions

You can withdraw any permission at any time in iOS Settings. The app keeps working with whatever remains — the live step counter runs even if you refuse Apple Health.

PermissionWhy
Apple HealthReading your step count and distance. Read only.
Motion & FitnessThe live step counter while you walk.
CameraScanning barcodes and taking food photos. No image is saved from a barcode scan.
PhotosChoosing a profile picture. We do not read your library; you pick one image.
NotificationsWater, step and measurement reminders. Entirely optional.

8. Your Rights

  • Access: You can review your data at any time within the app.
  • Correction: You can edit your profile and data directly from the Settings page.
  • Deletion: You can permanently delete your account and your personal data from inside the app — Profile → Delete account. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
  • Data in groups you joined: The rows you published into a step group or a challenge (your name and step figures) are held in that group, not in your account, and deleting the account does not remove them. Leave the group first if you want them gone, or email us and we will remove them for you.
  • A copy of your data: You can export everything stored in your account from inside the app — Settings → Privacy → Export my data. You receive a single JSON file, a standard format that other apps and services can read. It does not include the rows you published into groups (above); email us for those.

9. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from this age group. If we become aware that we have collected data from a child under this age, we will delete it promptly.
